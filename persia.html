Zoroastrian Divine Cosmology

THE GODS OF PERSIA

In the beginning was the choice. Light or Darkness, Truth or Lie. The Persian cosmos is a battlefield of principles, where every god is a force in an eternal moral war.

Before the First God

Before Ahura Mazda and Angra Mainyu drew their eternal battle lines, there was only Zurvan, infinite time itself. In the oldest layers of Persian cosmology, Zurvan existed alone in a void without beginning or end, performing a thousand-year sacrifice in longing for a son. When doubt crept into his ritual, two beings stirred in the womb of time: one born of the sacrifice's faith, the other of its doubt. From this single moment of cosmic uncertainty, the twin spirits of light and darkness were born together, and the great war of all existence began.

The Supreme Duality

The Two Primal Forces

Unlike Greek family trees or Egyptian cycles, the Persian cosmos is defined by cosmic moral opposition. every divine being aligns with one of two eternal forces.

The Light. اهورامزدا

AHURA MAZDA

Lord of Wisdom. the Uncreated, Eternal Good

Ahura Mazda

اهورامزدا. آفریننده و خیر مطلق

Supreme Creator God

The uncreated, self-sustaining Lord of Wisdom who existed before all time. Ahura Mazda is the source of all good in the universe. truth, righteousness, and light. He created the six Amesha Spentas as extensions of his own divine nature, each embodying a sacred principle. He is the cosmic legislator of Asha, the divine order that underpins all reality.

Vohu Manah

بهمن. اندیشه نیک

Amesha Spenta. Good Mind

The first and most intimate of the Amesha Spentas, Vohu Manah is the embodiment of Good Mind and Righteous Thought. He is the divine intellect through which Ahura Mazda first communicated with the prophet Zarathustra. As patron of cattle and beneficent animals, he guards the peaceful creatures of creation and leads souls at death to their final judgment.

Asha Vahishta

اردیبهشت. بهترین راستی

Amesha Spenta. Best Truth / Righteousness

Asha Vahishta personifies the highest truth, righteousness, and the divine order of the cosmos. He is the eternal law by which the universe operates. the principle that keeps fire burning truly and stars in their courses. As guardian of sacred fire, he is invoked in every fire ritual and represents the moral spine of Zoroastrian theology.

Mithra

مهر. ایزد پیمان و خورشید

Yazata. Covenant, Light, Justice

One of the most ancient and powerful of the Yazatas, Mithra commands covenants, contracts, and the binding oaths between men and gods. He drives his solar chariot across the heavens, preceded by Sraosha and followed by Rashnu. A god of war, justice, and friendship, Mithra's cult would later spread westward, becoming one of Rome's most important mystery religions.

Anahita

آناهیتا. الهه آب‌ها

Yazata. Waters, Fertility, Wisdom

Ardvi Sura Anahita, the Immaculate and Mighty, is the cosmic river goddess who flows from the mythical Mount Hara to nourish all waters on Earth. She purifies the seed of men and the wombs of women, grants warriors victory, and gives priests wisdom. Dressed in gold-embroidered robes with a beaver-fur cloak and a jeweled crown of eight rays, she is among the most magnificently described of all Persian deities.

Sraosha

سروش. فرشته پیام الهی

Yazata. Obedience, Discipline, Divine Word

Sraosha, whose name means Obedience or Hearing, is the divine messenger and the first god to worship Ahura Mazda. He is the personification of religious discipline and obedience to the divine will. Sraosha guards humanity against the demon Aeshma (Wrath) and guides souls through the three nights after death, protecting them on the Chinvat Bridge until they face divine judgment.

The Darkness. اهریمن

ANGRA MAINYU

The Destructive Spirit. the Uncreated, Eternal Evil

Angra Mainyu

اهریمن. روح ویرانگر

Supreme Destructive Force

The twin of Spenta Mainyu (Holy Spirit), Angra Mainyu. known as Ahriman. is the embodiment of destruction, falsehood, and all evil. He did not create but corrupts: for every good creation of Ahura Mazda, he produced a counter-creation of darkness. He is not omnipotent but has existed as long as Ahura Mazda, locked in a cosmic war that will eventually end in his defeat at Frashokereti, the final renewal.

Aeshma

خشم. ویرانگر

Daeva. Wrath, Violence, Outrage

Aeshma Daeva is the personification of unbridled wrath, violence, and the lust for war. He is one of the most powerful of Angra Mainyu's servants and the direct adversary of Sraosha. Aeshma incites war among nations, drives men to acts of brutal violence, and corrupts the righteous with rage. His name survived into later tradition as the demon Asmodeus of Jewish and Christian lore.

Aka Manah

اکومن. اندیشه بد

Daeva. Evil Mind, Corruption

Aka Manah, Evil Mind, is the demonic counterpart of Vohu Manah, Good Mind. He is the force that corrupts human thought, leading the pious astray through vain desires, false pleasures, and deception. He seduced the prophet Yima into sin and continuously works to distract humanity from righteousness. He is the inner voice of temptation and the corrupting principle of selfish thought.

Apaosha

اپوش. خشکسالی

Demon. Drought, Darkness, Famine

Apaosha is the fearsome demon of drought and darkness. a monstrous black horse that roams the earth drying up rivers and scorching fields. He is the eternal adversary of Tishtrya, the rain-star Sirius. Every year, their celestial battle is fought: if Tishtrya wins, monsoon rains fall and the earth is renewed; if Apaosha prevails, famine and death spread across the land.

Druj

دروغ. دروغ کیهانی

Cosmic Force. The Lie, Corruption

Druj. The Lie. is not merely a demon but a cosmic principle opposing Asha (Truth/Righteousness). Druj permeates all evil, all falsehood, and all corruption in the universe. She is a feminine force. sometimes depicted as the demon Jeh who corrupted the first woman. and represents the anti-principle to all divine order. To follow Druj is to align oneself with entropy and destruction.

Servants of the Light

The Yazatas

The Worthy Ones. divine beings created to be worshipped, each presiding over a sacred aspect of creation.

Verethragna

بهرام. خدای پیروزی

Victory, War, Heroism

The greatest of warrior gods and the embodiment of divine victory, Verethragna appears in ten different forms. wind, bull, white horse, camel, boar, youth, raven, ram, buck, and warrior man. He strikes down obstacles, enemies, and evil. Sacred to kings and warriors, he grants the kingly glory (xvarenah) that legitimizes rulers.

Tishtrya

تیشتر. ستاره باران

Star Sirius, Rain, Fertility

The magnificent star-god associated with the brightest star in the sky. Sirius. Each year Tishtrya descends to the cosmic sea as a white horse, where he battles Apaosha the drought-demon. Worshipped intensely by farmers and herders, his victory brings life-giving rain to a parched earth. He is the guardian of all living waters and agricultural prosperity.

Atar

آذر. ایزد آتش

Sacred Fire, Purification, Divine Presence

Atar is the sacred fire of Ahura Mazda. not merely a natural phenomenon but the living son and manifestation of the supreme deity himself. Every fire lit in worship is Atar made manifest. He fought the dragon Azhi Dahaka for the divine glory of Iran, retrieving it from the sea depths. As the eternal flame in Zoroastrian temples, he witnesses every oath and prayer.

Rashnu

رشن. ایزد دادگری

Divine Justice, Judgment, Balance

Rashnu is the righteous judge of the dead. the deity who holds the scales upon which souls are weighed at the Chinvat Bridge. Perfectly impartial, he cannot be bribed, deceived, or pressured even by the most powerful. He accompanies Mithra and Sraosha in judgment and is the personification of absolute fairness and cosmic justice.

Haoma

هوم. ایزد گیاه مقدس

Sacred Plant, Immortality, Healing

Haoma is simultaneously a divine being and a sacred plant. the intoxicating ritual drink pressed from a mountain plant in Zoroastrian ceremonies. He is the lord of healing, righteousness, and the granter of immortality. Haoma descends from the mythical mountain Haraiti, the world's center, and his pressed juice consumed ritually connects worshippers to the divine realm.

Spenta Armaiti

سپندارمذ. ایزدبانوی زمین

Amesha Spenta. Holy Devotion, Earth

The gentle daughter of Ahura Mazda and guardian of the sacred earth, Spenta Armaiti embodies holy devotion, serenity, and loving obedience to divine wisdom. She is the cosmic principle of religious piety and is associated with the fertile earth itself. Faithful women and virtuous wives are especially under her protection. she is the feminine face of divine grace.
